# Status Update Style

When the user asks to write, edit, or refine a status update:

- Always produce a **single paragraph narration**. No bullet points, no numbered lists, no headers within the status body.
- Keep it concise and crisp — remove filler words, redundant phrasing, and unnecessary context.
- Lead with the overall status (GREEN/YELLOW/RED) and the goal/deadline.
- Frame past launches as context for current metrics, not as "this week" events.
- Use inline date references (MM/DD) to anchor key milestones within the narrative flow.
- Group forward-looking work naturally at the end with clear ETAs.
- Maintain a confident, factual tone — no hedging language.
