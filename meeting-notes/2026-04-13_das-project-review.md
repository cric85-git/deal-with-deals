## AI interview training prototype demonstration

Matt presented a comprehensive interview training system he built that allows users to select interview types, experience levels, and evaluation criteria. The system generates mock resumes, conducts interactive interviews with AI candidates, and provides detailed coaching feedback on interviewing techniques and assessment accuracy. The prototype includes features for saving interview history and tracking performance over time.

## Technical implementation details

The prototype is hosted on AWS EC2 with a monthly cost estimate of $85, built using Kira as the development platform. Matt mentioned this represents his first experience setting up an EC2 instance and hosting a full prototype, demonstrating the accessibility of modern AI development tools.

## Potential applications for certification

The team discussed the prototype's relevance to DHE (Diversity, Hiring, and Excellence) certification processes, particularly for providing mock interview practice when actual interviews aren't available. There was also mention of real-world AI interview implementations, with one participant sharing that their friend recently experienced an AI-conducted interview at a Canadian organization.
